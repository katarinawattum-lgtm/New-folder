# Clicker Kingdom 🎮

## What is Clicker Kingdom?
Clicker Kingdom is a game where you collect gold and build your own kingdom. You can buy houses, hire workers and upgrade your buildings to make more gold.

## How to play

### Step 1: Get some gold
Click the **"Click here to earn gold!"** button to get gold. You get 1 gold every time you click it.

### Step 2: Buy a house
You need a house before you can hire a worker. A house costs 25 gold, so keep clicking until you have enough.

### Step 3: Hire a worker
When you have a house and at least 10 gold, you can buy a worker. Workers earn 1 gold every second.

### Step 4: Earn more gold
Your workers keep earning gold automatically. This makes it easier to save up for more houses and upgrades.

### Step 5: Upgrade your houses
When you have 100 gold, you can upgrade a house. An upgraded house can fit 3 workers instead of just 1.

### Step 6: Build your kingdom
Keep buying houses, hiring workers and upgrading your buildings. The goal is to keep growing your kingdom and earning more gold!

## How to open the game
1. Find the folder with the game files.
2. Make sure `index.html`, `style.css` and `script.js` are all in the same folder.
3. Open `index.html` in your browser.
4. Start playing!

## What I used
- HTML for the content
- CSS for the design
- JavaScript for the game mechanics

## Saving progress
The game saves your progress automatically in your browser, so you don't have to start over every time you open it.
